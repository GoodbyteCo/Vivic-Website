Vivic Research partnered with the Elizabeth Fry Society of Manitoba to conduct a social cost-benefit analysis of community bail supervision programs for women in Manitoba. Comparing the fiscal and social costs of pre-trial detention and community supervision, the research highlights the disproportionate benefits of relying on community-based alternatives that can disrupts cycles of criminalization. The research process included calculating daily costs for pre-trial detention and community supervision, as well as focus groups with women with lived experience to better reflect the social dimensions of this work. In addition to a research report, Vivic Research developed a short leaflet with the key findings.
